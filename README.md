# UB Restaurant

UB Restaurant brings the warmth of a Lagos kitchen to an easy, welcoming online ordering experience. Guests can discover Nigerian comfort food, browse the menu at their own pace, build an order, and choose an available checkout option from a phone, tablet, or desktop.

The experience is built around a simple idea: good food should feel close to home. From smoky party jollof and slow-simmered soups to grilled favourites, the website connects the restaurant's kitchen with customers looking for fresh meals and thoughtful delivery.

## The Experience

The storefront pairs food photography and a clear, warm visual identity with practical tools for finding and ordering a meal. The home page introduces the restaurant, highlights selected dishes, and points visitors toward the full menu. Customers can move from browsing to their bag without losing track of what they have chosen.

The site is designed to make the everyday tasks straightforward: find a dish, understand the offer, adjust quantities, place an order, and return later to check on it. Navigation keeps restaurant information, customer account tools, and help content close at hand.

## Explore the Restaurant

- **Home:** A food-led introduction to UB Restaurant, with kitchen highlights, featured meals, and direct routes to the menu.
- **Menu:** Browse dishes supplied by the restaurant, search by dish name or description, and narrow the selection by category. Availability is reflected in the ordering experience.
- **Food gallery:** Explore meal and restaurant imagery through category filters and search.
- **About:** Learn about the restaurant's approach to cooking, ingredients, hospitality, and delivery.
- **Services:** Discover the menu and place an order for the dishes available to you.
- **Contact:** Send a message to the restaurant team when you need help or want to get in touch.
- **Frequently asked questions:** Find guidance on orders, delivery, payment, accounts, cancellations, and support.
- **Privacy and terms:** Review the site's published privacy and service information.

## From Menu to Table

1. Browse the menu or search for a dish that sounds good.
2. Add meals to the cart and adjust quantities as needed.
3. Review the order and continue to checkout.
4. Complete an available online payment or choose cash on delivery where offered.
5. Sign in to your account to revisit order details and follow order updates.

The cart keeps selections together as customers explore. Clear quantity controls make it easy to change an order before checkout, while the checkout flow connects the confirmed order to the restaurant's kitchen and order-management tools.

## Customer Accounts

Customers can create an account, verify their email address, sign in, and recover access through the password-reset flow. The account area brings together an overview, personal profile details, and order history so returning customers have a useful place to manage their relationship with the restaurant.

Order pages give customers a place to revisit their purchases and see the latest status made available by the restaurant. The website also provides a payment-return page for customers completing an online checkout.

## A Connected Restaurant Team

The public storefront is supported by a dedicated, access-controlled admin experience. Restaurant staff with the appropriate role can review the business dashboard and work with incoming orders. Customer-management tools are reserved for the higher-privilege administrator role.

This separation keeps customer shopping focused on food and ordering, while giving the restaurant team a purpose-built place to follow the work behind each delivery.

## Thoughtful Details

- **Made for different screens:** Responsive layouts support browsing and ordering on mobile, tablet, and desktop.
- **Clear order controls:** Menu items can be added, removed, and adjusted directly from the browsing and cart experience.
- **Useful feedback:** Loading, empty, and error states help explain what is happening when menu data is unavailable or a search has no matches.
- **Customer support at hand:** Contact options, a WhatsApp shortcut, and a practical FAQ make it easier to reach the team.
- **Respectful motion:** Page transitions and visual effects are part of the experience, with reduced-motion preferences considered in key storefront views.
- **Secure online checkout:** Online payment is handled through the payment provider rather than asking customers to enter card details into the restaurant's own pages.

## The Website in One Sentence

UB Restaurant is a welcoming digital front door to a Lagos food business: a place to discover familiar Nigerian flavours, place an order with confidence, and stay connected with the restaurant from first visit through delivery.
